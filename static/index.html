A little timing note

Your LRC can use one timestamp per line, for example [00:15.24] Your lyric here. No word timestamps are required. Line-timed LRC estimates each word’s entrance; enhanced LRC uses exact word timestamps. Each new line starts a fresh phrase.

Manual timing workflow

Select a lyric line and press R. Playback starts just before the line and the first word is selected. Press T on every word you hear. Use Loop Line to repeat difficult phrases and Undo whenever a tap is off.
